# ITIL 4 Incident Management & SLA Guidelines

## Priority Matrix (Impact vs Urgency)
- **P1 (Critical)**: Production down for Enterprise account. Target Response: $< 15$ mins, Target Resolution: $< 1$ hour.
- **P2 (High)**: Impaired core functionality. Target Resolution: $< 4$ hours.
- **P3 (Standard)**: Non-critical question or cosmetic bug. Target Resolution: $< 24$ hours.
